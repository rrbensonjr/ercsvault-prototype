# ERCESVault — prototype

A working prototype of one ERCES compliance record, shown across the three parties who
work in it: the Authority Having Jurisdiction, the License Frequency Holder, and the
ERCES Integrator.

**It is a prototype, not a product.** Projects, buildings, dates and documents are
invented, and nothing here is saved — clicking around is the point. The screens are built
to be marked up and argued over, not used.

Open `index.html`, or the published page.
